| **Blog Post (`/blog/...`)**                  | 26          | 100           | 96             | 92  | 48.5s | 116.1s | 0.005 | 3,120ms |

#### Core Root Causes for Poor Baseline Performance:

1. **Artificial Delay in Preloader**: `Preloader.tsx` forced `setTimeout(..., 1900ms)` and rendered a full-screen fixed overlay.
2. **Blocking Skeleton Shell**: `BootSkeleton.tsx` fixed screen overlay `#dws-boot` with 0 opacity transition only after React hydration.
3. **Hero Component Render Delays**: `Hero.tsx` used `delayChildren: 1.9` and `SplitText delay={2.1}`, pushing LCP elements out past 3-4 seconds.
4. **Router Delays**: `defaultPendingMinMs: 350` in `router.tsx` inserted artificial delay on client transitions.

## 3. Issues Identified & Action Plan

1. **Brand Name Standardization**: Brand name is inconsistently written as "DwS" instead of "DWS Web Services" in metadata, title tags, schema, and footer.
2. **Canonical Absolute URLs**: All canonical tags are currently relative (`/about`), causing crawler ambiguity. Must be absolute HTTPS canonical URLs (`https://dws.co/...`).
3. **Meta Description Length**: Several pages exceed 155 characters, leading to SERP snippet truncation.
4. **H1 Alignment**: Homepage H1 must be "Website Design & SEO Agency in Jaipur". Inner service pages must contain primary keywords in H1 without stuffing.
5. **No Em Dashes & No Emoji Icons**: Per user guidelines, replace em dashes with clean hyphens or pipes, and remove emoji icons.
6. **Word Count & Structure on Service Pages**: Service pages need comprehensive sections (What is Included, Process, Who It's For, Pricing Range, 5+ Real FAQs, Clear CTA) reaching 500+ words of actionable content.
7. **Structured Data Validation**:
   - Organization + ProfessionalService on all pages
   - Service + FAQPage on service pages
   - BlogPosting on blog posts with valid schema
   - BreadcrumbList on inner pages
8. **Technical SEO Essentials**:
   - `public/robots.txt`: Disallow `/dashboard`, `/auth`, `/api/`, point to absolute sitemap URL.
   - Dynamic sitemap with `lastmod`, priority, and canonical URLs.
   - `public/google0b960ea3bfa41cfa.html` placed and accessible.
   - Google site verification meta tag in `<head>`.
   - `vercel.json` with HTTPS enforcement, HSTS, security headers, CSP, caching, and clean URL normalization.
   - New pages: Privacy Policy (`/privacy-policy`) and Terms of Service (`/terms-and-conditions`).
   - Breadcrumbs component and schema for inner pages.
   - Cal.com/Calendly embed slot + validated contact form with honeypot + floating WhatsApp button.
   - Single central configuration file `src/data/config.ts` for all business facts, social links, contact, keywords, and booking links.
